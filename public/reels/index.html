Reel Studio · Bike Logger · 60 fps · silent

One bike, many ways.

Reels for Bike Logger, made with Reel Studio. Each piece is one composition built from the same sourced facts (Raspberry Pi 5, Camera Module 3 Wide, GPS, BNO085, ultrasonic, A3144 Hall sensor) and rendered frame by frame in four looks. The pattern pack arranges those facts by 19 ad patterns. Tap a tile to play it.

Shown here: the main 9:16 cut of each piece and look. Every piece also exists in 4:5, 1:1, 16:9 and 1.91:1, in a fun and a dry adult voice and with three hooks. Rendered with Reel Studio's core/render.mjs; every file passed its video QA (30 fps CFR, H.264, safe zones).
